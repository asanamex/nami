# Commercial use of Nami

Nami is licensed under [PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0](LICENSE.md) (SPDX: `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`, source-available, not open source). That covers every noncommercial use: playing mods, building mods, distributing free mods, modifying Nami itself. Keep the Required Notice and the license text with anything you distribute.

## Paid mods and other commercial use

Selling a mod built on Nami, bundling Nami into a paid product, or any other commercial use needs a separate commercial license from the licensor. There is no standing commercial grant; each one is agreed in writing, per case.

To ask:

1. Open an issue at <https://github.com/asanamex/nami/issues> titled `Commercial license request: <your mod or product>`.
2. Describe what you are selling, where, and for how much.
3. Wait for written approval before you charge anything.

Commercial licenses include a credit term: paid mods state they are made with Nami. Free mods are asked (not required) to carry the same credit line; the `nami-mod` template ships with it.
