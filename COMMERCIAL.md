# Commercial use of Nami

Nami is proprietary software under the [NAMI LICENSE](LICENSE.md). Section 1 covers every noncommercial use: playing mods, building mods, distributing free mods, modifying Nami itself. Section 2 requires publicly distributed Nami mods to state they were made using Nami.

## Paid mods and other commercial use

Section 3 withholds all commercial exploitation (selling, licensing, renting, sublicensing, monetizing a Nami mod) without prior written permission. Each grant is agreed separately under section 4.

To ask:

1. Open an issue at <https://github.com/asanamex/nami/issues> titled `Commercial license request: <your mod or product>`.
2. Describe what you are selling, where, and for how much.
3. Wait for written approval before you charge anything.

Using Nami or mentioning it endorses nothing either way (section 5), and the license grants no trademark rights beyond attribution (section 6).
